Employee Handbook Leave Policy Guidelines
This leave policy document details the probationary requirements, working hours, and leave rules of Acme Systems Ltd.

1. Probationary Period
All new employees are subject to a probationary period of 6 months. During probation, employees are not eligible for paid casual leaves, but may take unpaid leave with prior manager approval.

2. Working Hours
The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, including a 1-hour lunch break. Regular attendance and punctuality are expected from all team members.

3. Paid Leave Allocation
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid casual leave per calendar year. Leaves are accrued at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused leaves up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year.

4. Sick Leave and Emergency Conditions
Employees are entitled to 7 days of paid sick leave per year for medical reasons. A doctor's certificate is mandatory for sick leaves extending beyond 2 consecutive days.

5. Maternity and Paternity Leaves
Maternity leave is provided for 26 weeks, and paternity leave is provided for 2 weeks, in compliance with standard national regulations.
